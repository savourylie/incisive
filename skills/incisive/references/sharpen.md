# Playbook: sharpen

Upgrade weak, obvious, or topic-only claims into incisive, contestable arguments, and align the beginning of the essay with its strongest point.

Load `references/calibration-examples.md` for claim and intro-conclusion alignment anchors.

## Workflow

1. **Locate the Real Point**:
   - Compare the introduction's thesis candidate against the conclusion's thesis candidate and every major section claim.
   - If the conclusion contains a sharper, more specific, or more surprising claim than the introduction, **promote it**.

2. **Test and Upgrade Against the 4 Criteria**:
   - Eliminate pure topic announcements ("This essay examines...").
   - Eliminate truisms no knowledgeable reader would contest.
   - Eliminate vague value adjectives ("very interesting", "extremely important", "one of the greatest"). Replace them with the *substantive mechanism, tension, or consequence* that makes the claim matter.
   - Narrow overly grand claims so they are provable within the essay's length.

3. **Execute Frame Alignment**:
   - **If `point-first` (default)**: Rewrite the closing sentence(s) of the introduction so the promoted main claim appears clearly and early, adjusting the surrounding intro sentences to lead into it naturally.
   - **If `point-last`**: Do not spoil the exact conclusion, but revise the end of the introduction into an *anticipatory launching sentence* that introduces the key conceptual terms and implicit questions the conclusion will answer.

4. **Check Downstream Alignment**:
   - Verify whether any section claim now contradicts or lags behind the sharpened main claim, and update those section claims accordingly.

## Deliverable

Show a crisp **Before -> After** comparison of the main claim (and updated intro/section thesis sentences), explain *why* the new claim is more contestable and substantive, and apply the edits to the draft.
