# Playbook: structure

Reorganize a sprawling, list-like, or summary-driven draft into 2-4 coherent, logically ordered sections where readers always know where they are and why they are moving forward.

## Workflow

1. **Audit Current Boundaries**:
   - Group existing paragraphs into 2-4 major logical sections (avoid fragmented micro-sections or a single undifferentiated wall of paragraphs).

2. **Enforce Fractal Section Design**:
   - Give every major section:
     - A brief **opening bridge** (1 sentence to 1 short paragraph) signaling the transition from the prior segment.
     - A clear **section-level claim** (default: point-first at the start of the section; if point-last, add an anticipatory opener containing the section's key concepts).
     - A brief **section wrap** only when the section is long enough to need consolidation.

3. **Replace Weak Ordering Principles**:
   - Diagnose and eliminate:
     - **Numeric listing** ("First... Second... Third...") where points have no substantive relationship to one another.
     - **Source-text shadowing** (following the original book/article's chapter order, which pulls the writer into summary).
     - **Drafting-diary order** (presenting ideas in the order the writer happened to think of them).
     - **Blind prompt mirroring** (mechanically copying the sequence of terms from an assignment prompt).
   - Reorder sections around a defensible analytical arc (e.g., surface appearance -> hidden mechanism -> systemic consequence; conventional view -> anomaly -> revised model; necessary condition -> sufficient condition).

4. **Verify Transitions**:
   - Rewrite section-opening sentences so each explicitly echoes the previous section's conclusion while launching the next claim.

## Deliverable

Present the new section architecture (section titles/roles + 1-sentence claim + explicit ordering rationale) before or alongside the restructured draft.
